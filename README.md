Sharpe-first paper-trading bot for a 6-hour pump.fun replay: many small, tight, low-variance trades. It buys young bonding-curve coins only on confirmed early momentum (price up from creation and still rising, all-buy flow from several distinct buyers, small first trade), sized at just 0.06 SOL with at most 5 open positions, and sells the whole position at +12% take-profit, −4% stop-loss, or an 800-slot timeout. Fully deterministic: every decision comes only from replay slots, prices and volumes.
